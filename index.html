PTSM 7392 Sports and Christianity in Historical Perspective

Interactive activity previews for faculty review · 16 activities, one per week

How to review: open each activity and try it as a student would. Then choose Use it, Don't use it, or Request a change under it, here or at the bottom of the activity, and add a note if you like. When you're done, select Copy my choices and paste them into an email to keith_pace@baylor.edu. Your choices are saved in this browser as you go. These previews don't record anything about how you do in the activities.
Week 1

Sport and the Church Through Time

Timeline

Eras from muscular Christianity to the modern era with Jackie Robinson as an anchor event and an embedded knowledge check.

Outcome: Explain and articulate the major ways scholars have defined history, sports and the Christian tradition.

Open activity
Week 2

Early Church Responses to Sport

Drag and Drop

Students sort 9 items into 3 categories (Used athletic imagery for the Christian life, Rejected the spectacles, Allowed moderate exercise), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Evaluate the theological principles and priorities that early Christians used as they engaged with sports.

Open activity
Week 3

Medieval Play, Festivals and Tournaments

Flashcards

Students practice 7 flashcards, recalling each before turning it; missed cards come back until recalled.

Outcome: Compare medieval Catholic Christian responses to sports with the early church.

Open activity
Week 4

Primary Source Analysis Practice

Guided Documentation

Students complete a guided form with 13 prompts in 5 sections, then copy or download their responses.

Outcome: Practice methods of historical and cultural interpretation and analysis.

Open activity
Week 5

Muscular Christianity, 1844–1895

Timeline

Students step through 6 timeline events, then answer a 4-question knowledge check.

Outcome: Evaluate the theological principles and priorities that leaders of muscular Christianity used as they engaged with sports.

Open activity
Week 6

Whose Muscular Christianity?

Dialog Cards

Students turn 5 cards and work through each card's reflection prompts, with an optional note.

Outcome: Explain how early muscular Christianity shaped understandings of sports, particularly for women and people of color.

Open activity
Week 7

Sport in the 1920s: Ally or Rival?

Drag and Drop

Students sort 8 items into 2 categories (Sport as an ally of faith, Sport as a rival to faith), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Evaluate how the approach to sports by American Protestants in the 1920s compares with today.

Open activity
Week 8

Rickey, Robinson and 1947

Dropdown

Students complete 7 blanks by choosing from dropdown menus, then check and fix any that are wrong.

Outcome: Explain how the cultural contexts of the mid-20th century influenced Christian understandings of sports.

Open activity
Week 9

The Rise of Sports Ministry

Drag and Drop

Students sort 6 items into 3 categories (1950s, 1960s, 1970s), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Evaluate the theological principles and priorities that early leaders in sports ministry used.

Open activity
Week 10

Jackie Robinson's Faith and Freedom

Drag the Words

Students drag 6 words from a word bank into a short passage, then check and fix any that are wrong.

Outcome: Evaluate the theological principles and priorities that Christian civil rights leaders used as they engaged with sports.

Open activity
Week 11

After Title IX: Theology or Culture?

Drag and Drop

Students sort 8 items into 2 categories (Theological principle or priority, Cultural context), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Explain how the cultural contexts of the 1970s and beyond influenced Christian understandings of women's participation in sports.

Open activity
Week 12

Course Names and Terms Crossword

Crossword

Students solve a 12-word crossword.

Outcome: Reflect on the ways the people and culture of sports in the present have been shaped by the past.

Open activity
Week 13

Comparing Four Approaches

Guided Documentation

Students complete a guided form with 16 prompts in 5 sections, then copy or download their responses.

Outcome: Compare and contrast the five different models of Christian sports engagement.

Open activity
Week 14

The Sunday Tournament

Branching Scenario

Students make choices through a branching scenario (5 decision points, 3 possible endings), with feedback after every choice.

Outcome: Evaluate the theological principles and priorities that Christians have used as they've engaged with modern youth sports.

Open activity
Week 15

Biography Research Planner

Guided Documentation

Students complete a guided form with 13 prompts in 4 sections, then copy or download their responses.

Outcome: Analyze how theology and cultural context shaped the life of the Christian leader you are researching.

Open activity
Week 15 · Final Week

Era-by-Era Exam Review

Flashcards

Students practice 11 flashcards, recalling each before turning it; missed cards come back until recalled.

Outcome: Prepare for the final exam across every era of the course.
